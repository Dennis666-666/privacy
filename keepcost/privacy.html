Keepcost Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Keepcost is a subscription tracker and spending log for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Summary

Keepcost does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data you enter

The subscriptions, amounts, dates, categories, notes and spending entries you add are stored only in the app's storage on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive your data, and Keepcost has no user accounts.

If you use your device's own backup (for example an iCloud or computer backup of your device), your Keepcost data may be included in that backup under Apple's terms. We cannot access it.

Shortcuts automations

If you choose to set up a Shortcuts automation that runs Keepcost's “Log Payment” action, the Shortcuts app passes the amount and merchant you configure to Keepcost on your device. Keepcost saves that entry locally. Keepcost cannot read your cards, your Wallet, or your bank accounts.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Keepcost schedules renewal reminders locally on your device. No reminder data leaves your device.

Exports

When you export a CSV file, the file is created on your device and shared only where you choose to send it.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a purchase is active.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Keepcost contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Keepcost is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries in the app, or delete all data by removing the app from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
