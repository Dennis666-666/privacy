Rowline Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Rowline is a pattern workbench and row counter for knitting and crochet on iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Summary

Rowline does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Everything you add stays on your device.

Pattern files you import

When you open a PDF or an image in Rowline (from the Files app, from your photo library, or with “Open in Rowline”), the app makes a copy of that file in its own storage on your device so it can show it later. The file is not uploaded anywhere. Rowline only sees the photos or files you pick; it does not ask for access to your whole photo library.

Rowline does not include, sell or share patterns. You are responsible for only importing patterns you own or are allowed to use.

Projects, counters and notes

Project names, counter values, highlight-ruler positions and notes are stored only in the app's storage on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive your data, and Rowline has no user accounts.

If you use your device's own backup (for example an iCloud or computer backup of your device), your Rowline data may be included in that backup under Apple's terms. We cannot access it.

Backup files

When you use Export Backup, Rowline creates one file on your device that contains your projects and pattern files. It is saved or shared only where you choose. Restoring reads a backup file you pick.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a purchase is active.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Rowline contains no analytics, no advertising, no AI services and no third-party SDKs. It works offline and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Rowline is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting a project removes its counters, notes and its copy of the pattern file. You can delete all data by removing the app from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
